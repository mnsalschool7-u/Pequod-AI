TeamPequod

Pequod AI is being built for operators who need trade software that is precise, practical, and calm under pressure. The mission is simple: help people coordinate global trade with better intelligence.

Building the coordination layer

Trade operations deserve software that thinks across the whole workflow.

Pequod brings customs, documentation, logistics, landed cost, and compliance into one workspace so teams can move from scattered files to clear decisions.

What the team is focused on

We are designing Pequod around the real messiness of international trade: PDFs, emails, portals, rulings, purchase orders, shipment data, and people working across companies.

The goal is not to replace judgment. It is to give trade teams a sharper workspace for finding facts, spotting gaps, comparing precedent, and choosing the next best action.
