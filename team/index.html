Pequod AI

Building customs exception intelligence.

Pequod AI is being built for importers, exporters, and customs professionals who need clearer answers when shipments are delayed, held, or difficult to classify.

What the team is focused on

We are designing Pequod around the moment an importer asks why a shipment is not moving. The product assembles customs notices, broker messages, product specifications, shipment documents, classification data, and tariff context into a structured workflow.

The goal is not to replace customs judgment. It is to help trade teams find the relevant facts, spot missing information, understand likely blockers, and prepare the next action faster.
